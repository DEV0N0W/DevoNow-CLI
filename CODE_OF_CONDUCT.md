# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

The DevoNow CLI community is committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and considerate.
- Discuss ideas and code without attacking people.
- Welcome questions and help others learn.
- Respect privacy and never publish another person's confidential information.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully, including requests to change scope or tone.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

The project does not currently publish a dedicated private conduct-reporting address. To report a concern, open a [minimal issue](https://github.com/DEV0N0W/DevNow-CLI/issues/new) asking a maintainer for a private contact channel; do not include conduct details or personal information in the public issue. Use the security advisory form only when the report also concerns a software vulnerability.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when behavior violates this policy.

Maintainers will handle reports as confidentially as practical and will consider context, impact, intent, and patterns of behavior when deciding on a response.
